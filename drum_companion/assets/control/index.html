Welcome to Drum Companion

Drum Companion listens to your live performance and follows its tempo while adding a drum groove. Choose Auto accompaniment or create and select your own named grooves.

On this computer, open the panel directly—no PIN is needed. From a phone, open the same page over your private Wi-Fi and enter the one-time PIN shown on the computer. Use Start and Stop for live control; the groove editor also offers a preview through the computer's audio output.

Access is limited to your private network. Use a trusted Wi-Fi network, allow the app through the firewall only on private networks, and do not expose or forward this panel to the public internet.
